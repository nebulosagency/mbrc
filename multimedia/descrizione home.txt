Maurice Bellanova is a multidisciplinary artist, performer and music producer. His practice explores the intersections between visual arts, sculpture, sound experimentation and performance, investigating the relationships between body, matter, sound and space.
He is co-founder of Cave Primitive, an independent artistic and cultural project combining artist residencies, creative production and interdisciplinary research. Conceived as a space for experimentation, exchange and collective creation, Cave Primitive supports artistic processes that move beyond conventional disciplines and established formats.
His sculptural and performative research explores unconventional forms, materials, gestures and symbolic imagery, creating situations in which physical, spatial and sonic dimensions converge.
Alongside his visual and performative practice, he develops RAGAZZACLAN, an independent music and performance project exploring hybrid and deconstructed sound territories, the project combines obsessive rhythms, tribal tensions, glitches and ritualistic atmospheres.
Through music production, performances and live sets, RAGAZZACLAN creates intense and destabilising sonic experiences, transforming the dancefloor into a space for collective expression and experimentation.
His work moves between contemporary art, underground culture and interdisciplinary practices, constantly searching for mutant, radical and evolving forms of expression.
